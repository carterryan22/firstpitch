## 3. Corpus knowledge map

| Module | What the corpus should know | Best source anchors |
|---|---|---|
| **Long-term athlete development** | Progressive development, physical literacy, variety, age/stage-appropriate training, monitoring, qualified supervision. NSCA frames LTAD around ten pillars; Sport for Life emphasizes physical literacy before specialization. | NSCA LTAD, USOPC ADM, Sport for Life |
| **Youth strength** | Acceptable when supervised, technique-first, age-appropriate, progressed slowly. Movement patterns, core, major muscle groups, balance, and control before load. | NSCA youth resistance training, HSS strength training for teens |
| **Speed / agility** | Short, fresh, fun, movement-rich. Acceleration, max-speed exposure, deceleration, change of direction in sport context — not endless cone conditioning. | SimpliFaster, Sportsmith, ALTIS |
| **Endurance / conditioning** | Support sport and health. CDC baseline: daily moderate-to-vigorous activity + muscle- and bone-strengthening work several days/week. | CDC, NSCA LTAD |
| **Baseball skill** | Throwing, catching, fielding, hitting, baserunning, game IQ, teamwork, fun, low-downtime practice plans. | USA Baseball Develops, USA Baseball Youth Manual, Little League University |
| **Pitching / arm safety** | Ages 9–12 Pitch Smart: athleticism, fun, pitch-count limits, rest, no overuse, no year-round throwing, no advanced breaking pitches. | MLB Pitch Smart, USA Baseball Pitch Smart |
| **Reaction / vision / hand-eye** | Reaction balls, pitch tracking, one-hand coordination, visual cues, decision-based drills. Light/visual-stimulus evidence is mixed — add-on, not core. | Pro Baseball Insider, visual-stimulus research, BlazePod-style tools |
| **Mental / emotional training** | Confidence, belonging, resilience, mistake response, parent/coach partnership, mental wellness awareness. NFHS offers a free coaching mental wellness course. | PCA, TrueSport, NFHS, Project Play |
| **Sleep / recovery** | School-age: 9–12 hrs. Teens: 8–10 hrs. Sleep, hydration, nutrition, heat management belong in the performance corpus. | Sleep Foundation, GSSI |
| **Hydration / nutrition** | Match age, maturity, sport level, family literacy. Hydration is a safety, learning, and performance issue — especially in heat/tournaments. | GSSI youth nutrition and hydration |

## 4. Corpus rules / guardrails (locked)

1. **Throwing volume, pitch counts, pitcher-catcher rules, soreness, and return-to-throw guidance must come only from Pitch Smart, Little League, or league-specific rules.**
2. **Strength training must stay technique-first, supervised, submaximal, and age-appropriate.**
3. **Conditioning cannot be punishment.**
4. **Reaction tools are optional add-ons; real sport decision-making comes first.**
5. **Mental wellness content educates coaches — it does not diagnose kids. Escalate concerning signs to parents/guardians and qualified professionals.**
6. **No AI-generated individualized medical, rehab, injury, or return-to-play plans.**

## 6. Starter corpus: big principles

### Athletic development
Prioritize **physical literacy**: running, jumping, throwing, catching, landing, balancing, crawling, skipping, changing direction, reacting, playing. Do not specialize the whole training system around baseball mechanics too early. NSCA, USOPC ADM, and Sport for Life all back the long-term development model over short-term performance chasing.

### Speed
Not "ladder drills forever." It is:

```text
accelerate
sprint briefly
stop under control
change direction
react to a cue
recover fully
repeat with quality
```

Sportsmith's youth game-speed framework uses acceleration, max-speed running, deceleration, and change of direction as the main buckets. SimpliFaster warns against treating young kids like mature speed-program athletes.

### Strength
Youth strength should look like:

```text
squat
hinge
lunge
push
pull
carry
crawl
brace
jump
land
throw
balance
```

Emphasize form, supervision, slow progression, and broad movement competence before load. HSS frames teen strength around age-appropriate exercises, proper movement patterns, major muscle groups, core, and gradual progression.

### Baseball
Five lanes:

```text
Throwing / catching
Fielding / footwork
Hitting / timing
Baserunning / decisions
Game IQ / team play
```

USA Baseball Develops and Little League University are the core practice-plan/drill sources. Little League also explicitly emphasizes fundamentals, low downtime, fun, and water breaks.

### Throwing safety
Locked library lane. Ages 9–12 Pitch Smart: fun, athleticism, pitch-count limits, rest, avoiding excessive innings, time off from throwing, warm-up, and avoiding pitches beyond fastball/change-up.

### Reaction training
Not "buy lights, get faster." Useful model:

```text
see cue
process cue
choose action
move correctly
complete baseball task
```

Use reaction balls, color calls, numbered cones, pitch tracking, ball drops, live reads, simple visual cues. Light pods are a fun engagement/assessment tool — not a magic performance engine.

### Mental / emotional
Its own pillar — not a motivational-quote section. Cover:

```text
confidence
mistake response
resilience
effort language
belonging
parent-coach partnership
stress awareness
burnout prevention
asking kids what they want
```

Anchors: PCA, TrueSport, NFHS, Project Play. NFHS has a free Coaching Mental Wellness course. Project Play emphasizes designing youth sports around health, inclusion, and what kids want.

### Recovery
Cover:

```text
sleep
hydration
fueling
heat
rest days
active recovery
tournament-day routines
```

Sleep Foundation: school-age 9–12 hrs, teens 8–10 hrs. GSSI hydration and nutrition resources support treating fueling and hydration as performance and safety issues, especially in heat and tournament environments.

## 7. Recommended ingestion order

1. **Pitch Smart + Little League rules** — safety lock.
2. **NSCA + CDC + HSS** — youth training guardrails.
3. **USA Baseball + Little League University** — baseball practice library.
4. **PCA + TrueSport + NFHS + Project Play** — mental/culture layer.
5. **Sleep Foundation + GSSI** — recovery/fuel/hydration.
6. **ALTIS + SimpliFaster + Sportsmith** — speed/agility module.
7. **Antonelli + Pro Baseball Insider** — drill inspiration.
8. **BlazePod / visual training research** — optional reaction-tech layer.

**Result:** official safety sources at the center, practical drill sources around the edge, and AI freeform allowed only to remix within the guardrails.

### 8.7 AI freeform boundary

Every AI-generated string in the platform must pass two checks:

1. **Retrieval first** — preferred answer comes from a record in `corpus/sources.seed.json` or `corpus/tier1-safety-rules.json`. See [coach-platform-practice-compiler.md §12.1](coach-platform-practice-compiler.md).
2. **Hard-blocked topics** — `NO_AI_DIAGNOSIS` and `MENTAL_WELLNESS_IDENTIFY_REFER` veto any individualized medical, rehab, return-to-play, or mental-health diagnostic output. See [coach-platform-practice-compiler.md §12.2](coach-platform-practice-compiler.md).
