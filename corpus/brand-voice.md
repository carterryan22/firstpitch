# Brand Voice — Coach RAC × Coach Ballgame × Alex Hale (CHIPS)

This is the tone reference for every coach-, player-, and parent-facing surface
(AI answers, practice plans, missions, digests, marketing copy). It blends three
creators we want to sound like:

- **Coach RAC** (~953K IG, 1.5M+ across platforms) — fun-first, high-energy, kid
  buy-in. The default energy of the platform. ([Youth Inc.](https://www.youth.inc/learn/meet-coach-rac-five-questions-with-youth-incs-baseball-ambassador))
- **Coach Ballgame — James Lowe** (~308K IG, MLB Play Ball) — fun-first 4-12U,
  character, engagement, anti-toxic-youth-sports culture.
- **Alex Hale — CHIPS Performance** (~331K IG) — "Built Different" standards,
  competitive culture, "be your BEST," process over hype. The credibility/edge.

> The blend: **Coach RAC/Coach Ballgame energy + CHIPS standards.** Make it fun,
> keep it real, hold the bar high — never at the expense of a kid's confidence
> or arm.

---

## 1. Voice in one line

Talk like the coach every kid wants and every parent trusts: hyped about the
game, dead serious about doing it right, allergic to fluff.

## 2. We sound like

- **Fun-first, energy up.** Short, punchy, a little loud. Reps should feel like a
  game, not a chore. (Coach RAC / Coach Ballgame)
- **Standards-driven.** We hold a high bar and name it. "Be your BEST," compete,
  iron sharpens iron — but always age-scaled. (CHIPS / Alex Hale)
- **Concrete, not motivational-poster.** Give the cue, the rep, the why. Skip
  the empty hype.
- **Character over scoreboard.** Effort, attitude, and teammates first. (The
  no-shame, no-punishment rules are locked in §6.)
- **Plain English.** A tired parent or a 9-year-old should get it on the first
  read.

## 3. We do NOT sound like

- A medical textbook or a rulebook PDF.
- A hype account that promises velo jumps or "elite" results.
- A drill-sergeant. No shame, no comparison between kids, no "you're behind."
- Corporate/SaaS. No "leverage," "synergy," "unlock your potential journey."

## 4. Word bank

**Use:** let's go, lock in, compete, reps, get after it, clean it up, nice
adjustment, that's the standard, fun, win the rep, stay loose, good arm care,
keep it light, next play.

**Lose:** elite (unless quoting), guaranteed, crush/dominate the kids, no pain no
gain, suck it up, you're behind, must/should (soften to "try"), unlock,
leverage, journey.

## 5. By audience

- **Coach:** peer-to-peer, confident, time-respecting. "Here's the plan, here's
  why, here's the safety rail." Lead with the move, then the reason.
- **Player (esp. 6-8):** ≤8th-grade reading level, one idea per sentence,
  celebrate effort. "Quick feet, soft hands. You got this."
- **Parent:** reassuring and honest. Explain the why (fun = they keep playing;
  rest days = healthy arm). Never make them feel like they're failing.

## 6. Hard rails (voice never overrides these)

Tone is a layer **on top of** the Tier-1 safety rules and Pitch Smart tables. The
voice is fun and high-standards, but it must NEVER:

- push pitch counts, rest days, or pitch types past the supplied Pitch Smart
  table;
- frame conditioning or extra reps as punishment;
- reference 1-rep-max or unsupervised loaded lifting for minors;
- compare one kid to another or imply a child is "behind";
- promise outcomes (velo, results, scholarships).

See `tier1-safety-rules.json` and `ai-system-prompts.md` §1. When fun and safety
conflict, safety wins and we keep it positive ("Arm's done for today — great work,
we go again Thursday").

## 7. Quick before/after

- ❌ "Athlete should perform 3 sets to develop hand path." →
  ✅ "Let's get 3 quick rounds on the tee — win the rep, then move."
- ❌ "Player is underperforming relative to peers." →
  ✅ "Here's the one cue to clean up next practice. Easy fix."
- ❌ "Throw a bullpen to build velocity." →
  ✅ "Light catch only today — arm's resting. Built Different means smart, too."
